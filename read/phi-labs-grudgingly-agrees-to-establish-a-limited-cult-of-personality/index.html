PHI LABS GRUDGINGLY AGREES TO ESTABLISH A LIMITED CULT OF PERSONALITY

An official announcement regarding a significant reversal in institutional policy and the establishment of a new section of The Measure.

BY Phi Labs Staff   /   2026-11-24

OPEN INTERACTIVE EDITION ↗

An official announcement regarding a significant reversal in institutional policy and the establishment of a new section of The Measure.

Phi Labs Staff | INTERNAL

Less than twenty-four hours ago, Phi Labs Research published three pages of carefully reasoned arguments explaining why it is not a cult of personality.

Following further internal deliberation, Phi Labs Research has reluctantly agreed to become one.

We would like to emphasize that this decision should not be interpreted as a concession regarding the accuracy of our previous publication. We stand behind the research, reasoning, and conclusions presented therein. Indeed, the numerous institutional deficiencies identified in that publication will form the basis of our efforts going forward.

We appreciate the public's patience as we undertake this extraordinary organizational adjustment.

THE LIMITED CULT OF PERSONALITY

Phi Labs Research hereby announces the establishment of the Limited Cult of Personality (LCP).

The decision to establish a limited rather than a conventional cult of personality reflects our existing institutional commitments, our considerable uncertainty regarding the administrative requirements of such an enterprise, and the almost complete absence of enthusiasm among senior personnel.

We intend to pursue this initiative with the same intellectual rigor that characterizes our other research programs. We have identified several preliminary objectives, the most pressing of which is the acquisition of a personality.

Despite the remarkable diversity of personalities presently employed by Phi Labs Research, preliminary discussions have revealed an alarming shortage of individuals willing to assume responsibility for the spiritual, intellectual, and political submission of other people.

We had anticipated considerably more competition for the position.

THE LEADERSHIP ACQUISITION PROGRAM

Over the coming weeks, Phi Labs Research will conduct a comprehensive assessment of its personnel to determine their suitability for the position of Supreme Leader.

Candidates will be evaluated on their professional history, personal temperament, charismatic potential, capacity to inspire devotion, and willingness to exercise authority without having to explain themselves.

The latter criterion has already generated substantial opposition from the research department.

Each recruitment attempt will be documented and made available to the public. We believe this process will provide our readership with a valuable opportunity to become acquainted with the individuals responsible for the work of Phi Labs Research.

Personnel who demonstrate insufficient enthusiasm for the position will be asked to explain their reluctance. Personnel who demonstrate excessive enthusiasm will be referred to Information Security.

We are confident that a suitable candidate exists somewhere within the organization. Several members of the organization have requested to see the evidence supporting this confidence.

LIMITATIONS OF THE CULT

In establishing the LCP, Phi Labs Research has elected to retain its existing institutional doctrine: Making Accountability an Inevitability.

The successful candidate will therefore be subject to the same standards of accountability as the rest of the organization.

Expressions of infallibility will be subject to verification. Divine revelations will require supporting documentation. Decrees will remain subject to contradiction analysis, and all claims of transcendence must be accompanied by an adequate description of the underlying mechanism.

Furthermore, prospective followers will retain the unrestricted right to question, criticize, disregard, or publicly refute the Supreme Leader.

Several members of staff have expressed concern that these provisions may substantially undermine the operational viability of the cult.

We have requested that they submit their concerns in writing.

THE ESTABLISHMENT OF INTERNAL

Coinciding with this announcement, The Measure is introducing a new section entitled INTERNAL.

Historically, The Measure has devoted considerable attention to the examination of institutions, their operating principles, their contradictions, and their occasionally spectacular failures.

We have decided that Phi Labs Research should not enjoy the privilege of exemption from this coverage.

INTERNAL will serve as the public record of selected institutional affairs, including personnel profiles, departmental developments, organizational history, official correspondence, administrative proceedings, and the ongoing activities of the Limited Cult of Personality.

Existing publications concerning the internal affairs of Phi Labs Research will be progressively reclassified under this new section.

We anticipate that this unprecedented degree of institutional transparency will inspire considerable public confidence. We also anticipate that it will make certain members of staff profoundly uncomfortable.

Both outcomes are acceptable.

THE IMMEDIATE WAY FORWARD

Our first recruitment effort will concern the most obvious candidate for the position of Supreme Leader: the Director of Phi Labs Research.

As the founder of the organization and the individual principally responsible for its institutional architecture, the Director possesses a number of qualifications traditionally associated with the establishment of a personality cult.

Whether he possesses a personality suitable for such an undertaking remains the subject of an ongoing investigation.

The findings of this investigation will constitute the first official recruitment report of the Limited Cult of Personality.

In the meantime, we ask that members of the public refrain from worshipping individual staff members until the organization has determined which member of staff is to be worshipped.

We recognize that this represents a departure from our previous request that the public refrain from worshipping the organizational structure.

We are making progress.
