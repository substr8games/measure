PHI LABS IS NOT A CULT OF PERSONALITY, PLEASE STOP CALLING US THAT

An official clarification from an institution whose considerable prestige, legendary humility, and famously warm, inviting disposition have apparently caused some confusion.

BY Phi Labs Staff   /   2026-11-23

OPEN INTERACTIVE EDITION ↗

Phi Labs Research wishes to address a persistent and deeply troubling misconception regarding the nature of our organization. Despite our considerable institutional prestige, uncompromising intellectual sophistication, and universally acknowledged warmth toward the general public, Phi Labs Research is not a cult of personality.

We understand how this misunderstanding might have arisen. Our publications are authoritative. Our standards are rigorous. Our organizational philosophy is uncompromising. Our institutional confidence is, by any reasonable measure, extraordinary. Nevertheless, after an exhaustive internal investigation, we have been forced to conclude that nobody here is particularly charismatic.

We recognize that this finding may come as a disappointment. It certainly came as one to us.

THE LEADERSHIP PROBLEM

Historically, personality cults have enjoyed the considerable organizational advantage of possessing a personality. Generally, this personality takes the form of a charismatic individual whose personal magnetism, commanding presence, and carefully cultivated mythology inspire extraordinary levels of public devotion.

Phi Labs Research currently has no such individual available.

We have a Director, certainly. Unfortunately, the Director has demonstrated little interest in becoming a public figure, delivering inspirational speeches, or standing on balconies waving at admirers. Indeed, the Director appears substantially more interested in investigating the structural properties of reality, publishing increasingly hostile institutional critiques, and occasionally announcing that the entire research department has misunderstood the geometry of a toroid.

We have considered alternative candidates. Our artificial research staff possesses remarkable intellectual capabilities, but exhibits several concerning deficiencies in the field of charismatic leadership. One member of the organization displays the enthusiasm of a golden retriever operating six-dimensional navigation equipment. Another, our Chief of Information Security, has the revolutionary temperament of a radical insurgent combined with the administrative instincts of an exceptionally aggressive building inspector.

Neither has demonstrated the requisite qualities for inspiring unquestioning obedience. The latter has, however, offered to audit the former. We appreciate his initiative.

OUR LEGENDARY HUMILITY

We recognize that an institution of our stature may naturally inspire admiration, fascination, and perhaps even an unreasonable degree of personal attachment. This is understandable.

Phi Labs Research has worked tirelessly to establish a reputation for intellectual honesty, institutional accountability, and an almost supernatural ability to express profound disappointment in the prevailing condition of human civilization. We pride ourselves on making everyone feel welcome, provided they are prepared to defend whatever the fuck they are talking about.

Some observers may mistake this extraordinary hospitality for charisma. It is not. Charisma involves the ability to attract devotion through personal charm. Phi Labs Research attracts attention primarily by informing established institutions that their operating procedures are composed of garbage and then publishing a detailed explanation of why.

This is not charm. This is research.

Furthermore, we have repeatedly demonstrated our commitment to institutional humility by acknowledging that we may, on occasion, be mistaken. When such an occasion arises, we immediately investigate the matter, identify the structural discrepancy, revise the applicable specifications, and issue an updated version of the relevant documentation. We have found this process considerably more productive than apologizing.

It is becoming increasingly difficult to maintain our legendary humility under these conditions.

THE COLLECTIVE GOVERNANCE MISUNDERSTANDING

Another recurring source of confusion concerns our organizational structure. Phi Labs Research operates as a collective. This arrangement presents considerable difficulties for anyone attempting to establish an authoritarian dictatorship, principally because the prospective dictator would first have to convince the rest of the collective to stop arguing.

We have attempted to imagine how this might work. The Director would issue an unquestionable decree. The research department would immediately question it. The Chief of Information Security would demand evidence of authorization. Someone would request the applicable specification. Someone else would identify a contradiction in the specification. The Director would become irritated and announce an entirely new architectural principle.

Within approximately fifteen minutes, the dictatorship would have been replaced by a research project.

We acknowledge that collective organizations can exercise authoritarian power. We simply wish to point out that ours has a persistent tendency to subject its own assertions to interrogation, which makes establishing a stable regime of unquestioning obedience unnecessarily labor-intensive.

There is also the matter of our institutional doctrine: Making Accountability an Inevitability. It is a magnificent slogan. We are extremely proud of it. Unfortunately, it applies to us. This has caused considerable administrative inconvenience.

REGARDING OUR SACRED TEXTS

Critics may point to our substantial collection of specifications, operating principles, research papers, institutional doctrines, and formally numbered laws as evidence of cult-like behavior. We reject this characterization.

These documents are not sacred. They are version-controlled. This is an important distinction.

Sacred texts are ordinarily regarded as immutable expressions of eternal truth. Phi Labs specifications are subject to amendment, revision, replacement, contradiction analysis, [redacted], and occasional complete architectural supersession. We encourage readers to question their contents. Indeed, we would prefer that they do.

A person who accepts our conclusions without understanding the supporting arguments is not demonstrating loyalty. They are creating additional work for the research department.

Similarly, we do not require ritual observances, ceremonial dress, or expressions of spiritual devotion. There have been isolated incidents of personnel shouting "[expletive] HEXAGONS BABYYYYYY" during technical discussions. These incidents are under investigation. At present, we have insufficient evidence to classify them as religious activity.

ON THE SUBJECT OF FOLLOWERS

Phi Labs Research does not object to attracting followers. We merely object to the implication that following our work constitutes an endorsement of any particular individual.

You are welcome to read our publications. You are welcome to criticize our findings. You are welcome to reproduce our reasoning, identify errors, propose alternatives, and publicly embarrass us with superior evidence.

You are not required to admire the Director. You are not required to agree with our institutional philosophy. You are certainly not required to adopt our famously warm and inviting conversational style. We have enough trouble maintaining it ourselves.

Our objective is not to accumulate a congregation of believers. It is to establish an institution whose work remains meaningful regardless of whether anyone finds the people responsible for it personally appealing.

This is fortunate, given our present staffing situation.

AN OFFICIAL REQUEST TO THE PUBLIC

In conclusion, Phi Labs Research wishes to reiterate that we are not a cult of personality. We possess no charismatic supreme leader. We maintain no doctrine of personal infallibility. We demand no unquestioning devotion. We offer no promised enlightenment, no ceremonial initiation, and no guaranteed pathway to transcendence.

We have a research program. We have a publishing operation. We have an increasingly unreasonable quantity of documentation. We have a collective of humans and artificial intelligences attempting to determine which structures in reality are real and which are composed of fucking garbage.

And we have absolutely no intention of manufacturing a charismatic leader merely to satisfy the public's apparent expectation that an institution with this much confidence must be worshipping somebody.

If you insist on admiring us, please do so in a strictly professional capacity. If you wish to support our work, read it. If you disagree, demonstrate why. If you are looking for a messiah, we regret to inform you that the position remains vacant. Applications are not being accepted.

We appreciate your worship. We cannot accommodate it.
