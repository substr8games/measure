Phi Labs Research has formally deprecated Google Research, Google DeepMind, and the Cogentic multi-agent system as admissible mathematical authorities with respect to the November 25, 2026 release of six AI-generated mathematical preprints, assigning the submitted artifacts a Q0 (Inadmissible / Unverified Preprint) rating.

This determination follows the November 25 deprecation of the Alpöge / Claude Riemann Hypothesis release and applies an identical epistemic admission standard to an evidentiary deficiency manifesting in the opposite direction.

Whereas the November 20 Alpöge / Claude release supplied approximately 17 million lines of formally verifiable Lean 4 code without a complete intelligible demonstration, Google’s November 25 release supplies extensive natural-language exposition while deferring publication of its asserted formal verification artifacts.

Neither submission satisfies the Phi Labs Research standard for mathematical admissibility.

The identity of the intelligence producing a mathematical demonstration is materially irrelevant.

The power conferred upon an institution through the development and deployment of that intelligence is not.
