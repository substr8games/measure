OPERATING PROCEDURE / 001

HOW TO TRACE THE WIRE

Start with ENTITY in the left column. Connect it to the corresponding answer on the right. Then connect that answer to AUTHORITY on the left. Continue alternating sides until you complete all five roles.

Both columns shuffle. The final line should be one glorious golden tangle: 10 nodes, 9 segments, 5 verified matches.

Click or tap nodes in order; drag from the last connected node to the next; or use Tab and Enter. Wrong selections don't lock. The investigation clock continues while the game is open or closed.

The background is a completely static hex grid throughout. At two matches, connection effects intensify; at four they become absurd. The fifth match is an incident.
