# The Measure Rev38 — Multi-section reader

Apply this overlay at the website repository root, after Rev37.

- Articles can be cross-filed in multiple sections using their existing `sections` array.
- Section archives show a given article whenever that section is a member of its `sections` array; a single article URL and record are retained.
- Directory and listing metadata show all filing sections, including Science / Humanities.
- The article's **FILED UNDER** line links every section individually.
- Legacy single-section records still work; Special Reports PDF cross-listing remains intact.
- Editorial-depth classification and colored dots are unchanged.

No change to classifieds, image viewer, masthead, rails, or Lower Register.
